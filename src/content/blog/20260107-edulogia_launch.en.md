**I was born a "nerd", thus with a deep passion for technology and computing.**  
I have always experienced the duality of the digital world and the Internet. On one hand, an incredible amplifier of possibilities, curiosity, and knowledge. On the other, its "dark side" made of services that seem to give us something, but in reality are built to capture our attention and use us: social networks, various types of video games, "public squares" that give space and echo to the less pleasant aspects of the human soul.

**I am a father of two children, aged 8 and 11.**  
I try to do my best to accompany them in exploring who they are, and what surrounds them. To prepare them for autonomy in the physical, emotional, intellectual, and also digital world. All worlds that require to be lived one bit at a time, with the right steps and the right stages.

**No one prepared us to be parents.**  
Educating is difficult, especially when you "learn by doing". Because unfortunately parenting is not taught in school, and there are very few occasions that "teach us to educate". With a little proactivity, resources and experiences can be found, but for the digital part it is very difficult. And if it's difficult for me, a nerd who is passionate about this world, imagine for the rest of the parents and the educating community.

**From the union of these three realizations, Edulogia is born.**  
It is the desire to put my technological and educational skills at the service of other parents facing the same difficulties. I enjoy experimenting and finding solutions at the intersection of these two worlds, but I know that not everyone has the same opportunity to do so, yet they would still like to have something "to start from".

Because the cell phone itself is not the problem. And neither is the Internet. One is just a piece of metal, the other is just a way to share information. The danger lies in the applications, the content, and the ways of access that these two things together make possible. And I am convinced that **providing clear, practical, and accessible knowledge to use them consciously** is an important step to make what appears complex manageable. To parents, to families, to schools, to educating communities.

**To EDUcate on techNOLOGY. Together.**
